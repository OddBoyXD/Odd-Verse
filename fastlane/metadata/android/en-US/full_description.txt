Odd Verse is a modern, high-performance, and completely ad-free music streaming app engineered for pure speed, audio fidelity, and privacy.

⚡ Key Features & Highlights:
• Instant Playback Start (150ms): Custom low-latency ExoPlayer audio engine for zero-wait music playback.
• 5 MB Network Burst Buffering: High-speed caching pipeline engineered for stutter-free playback through elevators and subway tunnels.
• Stale-While-Revalidate Home Feed: Caches your homepage and playlists in local AES-encrypted Hive storage for instant 2ms loading.
• Real-Time Synced Lyrics & Transliteration: Karaoke-style synchronized lyrics with automatic phonetic Romanization for Hindi, Punjabi, and Gurmukhi.
• Local & Offline Music Engine: Automatic scanner for internal storage and MicroSD audio files (.mp3, .m4a, .flac, .wav, .aac, .ogg, .opus) with ID3 tag and artwork extraction.
• Artist Hub Sorting: Instant client-side sorting by Newest, Most Popular / Views, Oldest, and Alphabetical.
• Integrated SponsorBlock: Automatically skips non-music intros, sponsors, and outro banter silently.
• Lossless M4A Audio Export: Package and share clean audio files with embedded tags and album artwork.
• Full .ovb Backup & Restore: Encrypted single-file backups of your settings, playlists, and favorites.
• 100% Zero Ads & Telemetry: No video ads, no banner popups, no trackers, and no account logins required.
